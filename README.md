# 🥛 Sumul Dairy: Consumer Milk & Dairy Market Analysis

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard that turns survey data from **5,404 dairy consumers** into clear, actionable insights on brand preference, customer satisfaction, buying behaviour and product portfolio, built around **Sumul** (Surat District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union) and its competitors.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Dairy is a highly competitive, price- and trust-sensitive market. This project answers the questions a brand team would actually ask:

- Which brands do consumers prefer, and **why**?
- How satisfied are customers with Sumul, and does product quality drive that satisfaction?
- Where do people buy milk, how much do they consume, and how much do they spend?
- How does Sumul's product range compare with Amul and Mother Dairy?

The report has **5 interactive pages** with slicers on every page, so any segment can be explored in a click.

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. **Sumul leads the market in this survey.** It is the top preferred brand with ~**27.8%** share, ahead of Amul at ~**20.7%**, across 7 brand groups.
2. **Taste and quality drive brand choice.** Taste (924) and Quality (901) are the top reasons, well ahead of Nutrition (473) and Hygiene (446).
3. **Satisfaction has headroom.** Only **42.36%** of respondents are satisfied with Sumul, while **29.11% are "Not Sure"** and **27.55% are dissatisfied**. The undecided group is the biggest conversion opportunity.
4. **Quality perception is not translating into satisfaction.** Satisfaction stays at a similar level across Excellent, Good, Average and Poor quality groups, which suggests other factors (availability, price, service) also matter.
5. **Price is a perception battle.** About **61.5%** see prices as affordable/reasonable, but **~38.4%** still call them expensive.
6. **Retailers dominate purchasing.** Retailer is the largest purchase channel, followed by doorstep delivery, so distribution strength is critical.
7. **Pouch and mixed buyers dominate.** Around **41.8%** buy both pouch and loose milk and **~39.5%** buy pouch milk only, while loose-only buyers are a small share (~9.8%).
8. **Heavy consumers form the core base.** **61.84%** fall in the High Consumption segment, and **23.74%** are High-Value customers.
9. **Most households spend ₹1,000–₹2,500 a month** on milk and dairy.

## 💡 Recommendations

- **Convert the "Not Sure" group** (~29%) with sampling drives, trials and quality-focused communication.
- **Lean into taste and quality** in messaging, since these are the top reasons consumers choose a brand.
- **Strengthen retailer and doorstep presence**, the two biggest purchase channels.
- **Address the price perception gap** with value packs and pricing communication for the ~38% who find it expensive.
- **Target high-value, high-consumption households** with loyalty and subscription offers.
